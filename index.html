02How it started

It began
with a trend(really)

I didn't plan to become a creator. I started a trend on TikTok, watched it take off, and fell in love with making things. Every video after that, I got a little better.

Then a friend said I could dress better.

Thanks, Daniel. He was right, and that's how fashion became part of the story. I wanted to level up my style, so I started documenting it.

It got serious when I realized I had a real passion for creating, and for making something that gives people value, not just views. Now I concept, shoot and edit every piece myself, so the idea you sign off on is the idea that ships.
