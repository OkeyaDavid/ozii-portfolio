Profile

Toronto · GTA

Most creators hand a brand footage. I hand them the finished idea.

I'm Ozii, a Toronto-based fashion and lifestyle creator specialising in meaningful storytelling and editorial content for social media platforms. I came up through fashion, but I approach content the way an editor does: hooks, pacing, and a cut that earns the next three seconds.

Because I shoot and edit everything myself, a brief doesn't get diluted passing between hands. Concept, direction, filming, color and the final export all sit with one person, which usually means fewer rounds, faster turnaround, and work that looks like what we actually talked about.

My audience skews 18–34 and sits mostly in Canada and Nigeria. It's a small, attentive room rather than a big indifferent one.
